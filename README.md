**Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis
****Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using Python, SQL, and Power BI to identify sales trends, customer preferences, and business insights.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem Statement

Retail businesses generate large amounts of customer data, making it difficult to identify purchasing patterns and sales trends. This project helps analyze customer behavior, product performance, and revenue trends for better business decision-making.
